# Customer Data Analysis & Segmentation

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer data to understand customer demographics, age distribution, gender distribution, location patterns, and customer interests.

The project includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), visualization, and extraction of meaningful business insights using Python.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer demographics
- Understand customer distribution by country and state
- Analyze gender distribution
- Calculate customer age and age groups
- Analyze customer interests
- Identify important patterns and trends
- Perform basic data quality checks
- Generate meaningful business insights

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook
- Excel

## 🧹 Data Cleaning

The following data cleaning tasks were performed:

- Converted DOB into datetime format
- Calculated customer age
- Created age groups
- Standardized gender categories
- Checked for missing values
- Checked for duplicate records
- Analyzed categorical values

## 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis

The following analyses were performed:

- Customer distribution by country
- Customer distribution by state
- Customer distribution by gender
- Customer distribution by age group
- Customer distribution by interests
- Gender distribution by age group
- Gender distribution by state
- Gender distribution by country
- Interest distribution by gender
- Interest distribution by age group
- Age group distribution by country
- Average age by gender
- Average age by state
- Average age by country

## 📈 Key Insights

- The dataset contains 21 customers, with 19 from India and 2 from Nepal.
- There are 12 female customers and 9 male customers.
- The average customer age is 36.9 years.
- Customer ages range from 22 to 50 years.
- The 31–40 and 41–50 age groups are the largest customer segments, with 8 customers each.
- Delhi has the highest number of customers, with 4 customers.
- Female customers are more numerous in the 31–40 and 41–50 age groups.
- Female customers have an average age of 38.42 years, compared with 34.89 years for male customers.
- 17 out of 21 customers have their interest listed as "Not Specified".
- The dataset contains no duplicate rows.

## 💡 Conclusion

The analysis shows that the customer base is primarily concentrated in India, with a slightly higher proportion of female customers. Most customers are between 31 and 50 years old. Delhi has the highest customer representation among the listed locations.

However, interest information is largely unavailable, so collecting more complete interest data could improve future customer segmentation and marketing analysis.
